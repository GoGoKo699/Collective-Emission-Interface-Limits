pulse-capture framework with a uniform comparison of whole-code transfer and mean
photon collection. This directory identifies the inherited ingredients, the closest
constructions, and the source versions supporting the physical assumptions.

## Attribution and the scientific distinction

Paulisch's source analysis supplies the emitted cascade, conventional-pulse overlap,
useful-channel product and number-state mapping context. The conventional cubic
correction already identifies the relevant excitation scale. Law–Lee supplies
natural-mode optimization, the oscillator comparator, dominant two-mode occupation
and the semiclassical pulse. Kiilerich–Mølmer and the passive-memory sources supply
selected-mode full-state calculations and capture constructions. Tziperman and the
other state-mapping sources give directly relevant full quantum-state transfer
constructions with their own input, approximation and mode-selection rules.

The result established in the [scientific account](../science/README.md) fixes the
source and canonical target, selects one waveform before an unknown input, and
optimizes its worst-input squared entanglement fidelity over a growing number code.
The [matched-pulse estimate](../science/MATCHED_PULSES.md) controls the full emitted
isometry uniformly. The [common-mode theorem](../science/COMMON_MODE_BOUNDARY.md)
then provides a construction and a converse over every common waveform; the
[collection bound](../science/PHOTON_COLLECTION.md) quantifies the different domain
in which the mean photon fraction tends to one.

## Learning source and research references

The selected educational anchor is **Kiilerich and Mølmer, *Quantum interactions
with pulses of radiation*, Physical Review A 102, 023717 (2020)**,
[author version arXiv:2003.04573v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.04573v1).

Their roles are specified in the [background account](BACKGROUND.md).

## Evidence scope

The source register distinguishes inspected primary sections from abstract or
bibliographic evidence, identifies approximate and conditional preparation
resources, and separates theory from experimental component examples. Each comparison
applies to its stated source versions and constructions. These records support the
attribution and model interpretation; they do not establish an exhaustive literature
survey or convert component demonstrations into a realization of the complete
asymptotic interface.

The [verification guide](../verification/README.md) documents the finite checks
supporting the local analysis. The [physical scope](../science/PHYSICAL_SCOPE.md)
states the loss, source-rate and capture-control requirements used with the theorem.
