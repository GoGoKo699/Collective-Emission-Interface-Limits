## Preparation premise

Preparing selected known targets and providing an input-independent preparation isometry on the entire code are different resources. A heralded branch preserves a code when its action is proportional to an isometry on that code. Selected-target controls and their success probabilities are described in the [preparation note](PREPARATION_EVIDENCE.md).

analytic transfer, loss and capture results.
