# Assumption and resource register

**3 October 2026. Audited by premise, not by a combined citation count.** The [primary-source register](SOURCE_EVIDENCE.md) supplies exact locations, access depth, and limitations. F means relevant primary full-text sections inspected; A means abstract only. These are author-side literature checks, not independent proof review or a claim of a completed apparatus.

## Community precedent versus physical realization

| Premise | Role and direct evidence | Status and remaining boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Identical effective two-level constituents on the symmetric collective ladder | Source model. **7 F** papers S01-S07, including two PRLs, explicitly formulate the collective model or realize its small-system mechanism. | The community-model threshold is met. A prototype with two emitters is not evidence of homogeneous coupling and negligible symmetry errors over our full growing code. |
| One useful vacuum Markov channel with collective lowering | Effective-model convention. **6 F** formulations S01-S06; S07 is supporting finite-device evidence, not an exact lossless bath. | The theoretical convention is well grounded. Zero unwanted decay is an ideal limit. All useful photons belonging to one spatial channel does not mean one temporal mode. Finite delay, bath bandwidth and omitted channels require a compatible scaling family. |
| Arbitrary symmetric number superpositions available as inputs | Conditional input premise. **3 F** preparation papers S01, P01, P02; **2 A** related conditional preparations P03, P04. | Not a completed five-full-text construction audit. None of this register establishes uniformly accurate preparation of every growing-code state, including entanglement with a reference, in the proposed emitting device. Fock loading alone does not settle superposition preparation. |
| Known emitter number N | A calibrated model parameter in S01-S07, not a measurement supplied by their ideal equations. | Fixed N is a conventional conditional premise. No calibration precision or immunity to atom-number fluctuations is established for the complete device. The asymptotic theorem is a family of known-N tasks, not an assertion of a fixed experimental apparatus. |
| Predetermined passive linear receiver, vacuum auxiliaries, one retained oscillator | Defined resource class. **3 F** direct formalisms R01-R03; **1 F** related linearized memory model R04. **3** component/task experiments R05-R07, of which R05 is abstract-only here. | The receiver task is physically motivated; the theorem's network reduction is an application of linear input-output theory. The experiments are not seven demonstrations of the complete stipulated receiver, nor evidence that nonlinear or multimode decoding is impossible. |
| Ideal capture over all normalized waveforms, with no fixed control/time budget | An upper-envelope optimization. R01-R03 derive ideal capture; R02 explicitly records singular onset and finite-control truncation. | No finite device is claimed to attain the entire supremum. The source and receiver audits retain bandwidth, loss and duration costs. Component absorption does not certify arbitrary-code fidelity. |
| Canonical unconditional number map and worst-input entanglement fidelity | Mathematical task and performance definition, specified in [THEOREM.md](../research/THEOREM.md). The reference is an untouched diagnostic system. | Not an empirical assumption to validate by five laboratory papers. No postselection, optimized encoding, quantum-capacity claim or unrestricted nonlinear recovery is implicit. Preparing a reference-entangled input remains a separate physical task. |
| Optional independent uncollected atomic decay | Separate source audit. **5 F** papers S01-S03, S05-S06 formulate unwanted local decay alongside collective dynamics; A01 gives the collected-branch product. | This extension is not silently inserted into the ideal theorem. The recorded finite bracket is not a solved noisy optimum. Holding the effective useful-to-loss ratio fixed while increasing N requires justified microscopic rate control. |
| All premises realized simultaneously at growing N and code size | A prospective implementation claim, not needed to define a conditional theorem. | **No joint realization established.** Separate component papers cannot be added together to prove one apparatus. No uniform microscopic field-error or whole-code preparation theorem has been imported from one-excitation tests. |

## What this audit closes

The collective Markov-source premise now has more than five precise primary precedents rather than broad topic labels. The receiver has a documented linear-theory basis and separate experimental task examples. The [comparison](COMPARISON.md) explains why existing universal linearized storage results do not automatically control our finite-spin source, while retaining their actual quantum-state scope.

## What it does not close

The complete source-preparation audit has three inspected full texts, not five. The physical cost of arbitrary growing-code preparation, known-N calibration, and a consistent joint apparatus remains unestablished. Law-Lee full-text priority and a genuinely separate critical reading remain open. These deficits are not a reason to invent another Hamiltonian or count unrelated experiments. The appropriate claim remains a conditional source-to-memory theorem with explicit resources.

The original P1-P9 attribution labels remain in [PRIOR_ART.md](PRIOR_ART.md); the expanded register uses S, R, P and A labels only locally, with no changes to the canonical proof's reference numbering.
