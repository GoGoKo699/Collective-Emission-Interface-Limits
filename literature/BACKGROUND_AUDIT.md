## The newly open close-source task

**Offek Tziperman and coauthors, *Nonlinear Quantum Light Generation in Collective Spontaneous Emission*, ACS Nano 19, 21260–21270 (2025), DOI 10.1021/acsnano.4c15257.** The associated preprint is [arXiv:2306.11348](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.11348), titled *The quantum state of light in collective spontaneous emission*.

The primary abstract claims preservation/transfer of emitter correlations to traveling quantum light, selected single-mode cat and GKP states, and multimode treatments involving position, loss, interactions and non-Markovian dynamics. This makes it a required comparison rather than an optional broad review citation.

The approximately 15 MB v2 arXiv PDF exceeded the available text-retrieval size. Tested author-HTML, alternate arXiv and publisher full-text routes did not yield a readable complete construction; the accessible ACS supplement record does not substitute for the main paper. These retrieval failures do not imply the article is unavailable to a reader with other legitimate access.

A completed comparison must identify its source ladder, actual mode-selection/optimization rule, input state class, target map, fidelity metric, finite versus uniform asymptotic claim, and allowed receiving resources. It must check whether one receiver is chosen before an unknown/reference-entangled input and whether any all-waveform converse follows. The abstract alone cannot answer those questions. Do not dismiss it because its illustrative encodings or number of emitters differ, and do not claim that its positive examples contradict our restricted theorem without checking their premises.
